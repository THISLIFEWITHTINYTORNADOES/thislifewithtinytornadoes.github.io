Welcome to our little corner

Where somebody is probably barefoot, something is probably sticky, and dinner is almost definitely being reheated.

Hi, I'm Rachel

Mom, homemaker, baker, maker of things, and occasional referee.

This is where I share the real-life stuff I love: raising my kids, making our home feel like home, baking, finding fun things to do on a budget, celebrating the seasons, and figuring things out as I go.

It's not a perfectly organized life. It's a good one, though. Usually loud. Frequently covered in crumbs. Full of little moments worth remembering.
